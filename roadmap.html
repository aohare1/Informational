Stage 1 — Concept & Idea

Every game starts with an idea. At this stage, define your genre, core hook, target audience, and platforms. Keep your first project small — a simple finished game beats a complex abandoned one.

  • What genre is your game?
  • What makes it unique?
  • Who is your target audience?
  • What platforms will you target?
